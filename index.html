FIELD MANUAL
01 · FLY

Use WASD or the arrow keys to fly. On mobile, drag the joystick in the direction you want to move. Release the controls to coast. Gravity pulls you down, so brake with upward thrust before landing.

02 · DRILL

Push against a block to extend your drill automatically. You can mine left, right, or down—never up. The drill works on one block at a time. Dirt clears the way; minerals fill your cargo hold.

03 · FUEL

Watch the second gauge at the top. Flying uses a little fuel; drilling uses more. Below 15%, an alarm warns you to return. Dock at the left pad and tap the fuel depot to buy 1, 5, or 10 litres, or fill your tank. Running empty damages your hull.

04 · SURFACE SERVICES

There are three stops on the surface: fuel on the left, ore exchange in the middle, and garage on the right. Land close to the stop you need, then click or tap its highlighted building. Sell ore for credits and spend them on fuel, repairs, and upgrades.

05 · CARGO

Click the Cargo gauge, third from the left, or press C to open your hold. The bar shows whichever is closer to its limit: slots or weight. Check each mineral’s value, then choose Drop 1 or Drop All. Heavy loads slow you down. When every slot is full, you can still drill, but new ore is lost.

06 · HULL

The first gauge at the top shows your hull health. Hard landings and gas explosions damage it. Slow down before touching the ground, repair at the garage, and buy Suspensions to soften impacts and blasts. Below 25%, the gauge pulses red. Below 15%, smoke signals critical damage.

07 · SPECIAL TILES

Gas pockets appear below 1,000 metres and become more common deeper down. Look for colored bubbles inside the rock: blue is weakest, green is stronger, and purple is strongest. Mining a gas-marked block has a 20% chance of triggering a blast. Bedrock cannot be destroyed. Fractured basalt resists the drill but breaks in nearby gas blasts.
