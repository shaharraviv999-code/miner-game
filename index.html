FIELD MANUAL
01 · FLY

WASD or arrow keys move your craft. On a phone, use the floating joystick. Gravity pulls you down; long falls damage your hull.

02 · DRILL

Push left, right, or down into rock to mine it. You cannot drill upward. Dirt pays nothing; deeper ores pay more and resist your drill.

03 · WATCH YOUR FUEL

Fuel drains slowly while idle and faster while drilling. Below 15%, red warning lights pulse. An empty tank damages your hull.

04 · RETURN TO THE PADS

Fuel depot on the left. Ore exchange in the middle. Garage on the right. Dock close to each building to use its services.

05 · MANAGE YOUR LOAD

Click Cargo or press C to inspect and drop ore. Both slots and weight matter. An overloaded craft moves very slowly.

06 · INVEST IN YOUR CRAFT

Buy stronger drills, efficient cooling, larger tanks, hull plating, and cargo racks. Cyan seams warn of explosive gas. Esc pauses your shift.
